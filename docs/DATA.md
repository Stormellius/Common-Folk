# Data files and mods

Everything in the game that can be a list lives in `data/` as JSON. Each file is an array of entries, the file name is the kind of content, and every entry has a unique `id`:

```json
[ { "id": "grass", "name": "Grass", "color": "#6b9a45", "style": "grass", "priority": 2, "walkable": true, "move_cost": 1.0 } ]
```

## Mods

A mod is a folder in `mods/` (or `user://mods/` in an exported game) with the same kinds of files, and an optional `mod.json` (`name`, `enabled`, `load_order`).

- A new `id` adds an entry; an existing `id` replaces the whole base entry, so repeat all its fields.
- `{ "id": "water", "_remove": true }` deletes an entry.
- A file with a new name adds a whole new kind of content.
- Problems (bad JSON, missing ids) are shown on screen and printed as warnings.

`mods/example_mod` turns grass bright green; set `"enabled": true` to try it. In an exported game, add `*.json` under **Resources > Filters to export non-resource files**.

## The world

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `tiles.json` | `color`, `style` (`flat`, `grass`, `dirt`, `water`, `stone`, `forest`, `cobble`, `bridge`, `bridge_h`: picks the shader texture), `priority` (higher tiles bleed over lower ones' edges), `walkable`, `move_cost` |
| `seasons.json` | `days`, `foliage_tint`, `snow` (0 to 1), `rain_chance`, `cloud_chance`, `fireflies`, `need_drain_multipliers`. A season looks like itself for two thirds of its length, then eases toward the next |
| `worldgen.json` | `region` (map `width` and `height`, and the `settlements`: a `profile`, where (`at`, as fractions of the map) and `home: true` for the player's town), then one profile per kind of settlement (below): `buildings`, `npc_count`, size and `prices` (a multiplier on what its market pays for each good) |
| `buildings.json` | Templates drawn in characters: `#` timber wall, `S` stone wall, `+` door, `.` floor, space for nothing, and a letter for an object (b bed, p pot, h hearth, w basin, c chamber pot, l lute, t table, k counter, s shelf, r barrel, n bench, W well, m tavern table, a ale barrel, f bread shelf, B notice board, A altar, P pew, H sickbed, F forge, K workbench, T tool rack, L loom). Door in the bottom wall; the generator mirrors and turns it to face the street. `kind` (a `house` is filled with a family, one person per bed), `floor`, `public`, `class` (`gentry` for grand houses) |
| `floors.json` | Floors the player can lay: `color`, `line`, `pattern` (`planks`, `flags`, `straw`), `cost` per tile, `skill` it is laid with |
| `resources.json` | The things folk gather: `name`, `price` (what the shop sells it for; it pays `sell_percent` of that, from `economy.json`), `stocked: true` (the shop's counter sells it at that price: a ring), `trade: true` (brought by caravans, not made here) |
| `roofs.json` | Roofs the player can plan over a closed room: `materials` a tile, `minutes` a tile, `skill`, `color` and `edge` |
| `shelter.json` | `roofed_drain_multipliers` (needs that drain more slowly indoors) and `open_sleep_rest` (a share of its rest a bed gives with no roof over it) |
| `caravans.json` | `max_active`, how often one sets out (`spawn_chance_per_day`, `first_day`), `stay_days`, `move_ticks`, the `fare` and `max_passengers`, `wares` (a `good`, a `qty` range and a `weight`) and how many it brings, `sells_markup` and `buys_markup`, the goods it may be looking for (`wanted_pool`), `wanted_bonus`, how many units it will take of those it wants and of the rest (`wanted_demand`, `other_demand`), and its `names` |
| `names.json` | `given_female`, `given_male`, `family`, `town` and `pet` name lists |
| `appearance.json` | Skin, hair and clothing colours |

A settlement profile has `kind` (`town`, `village`, `hamlet`), `npc_count`, `buildings` (how many of each kind: `tavern`, `shop`, `clinic`, `chapel`, `park`, `house`, `vacant`), the clearing's `half_width`, `half_height_up` and `half_height_down`, `streets` (`ns` and `ew` offsets of side streets from the centre), `plaza` (a paved square with a well and stalls), `lake` (a pond and pier), and for the player's town `home_lot_size`. `vacant_lot_size` sizes the empty lots.

## Things and people

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `objects.json` | What can stand on the map. `cost` (to build), `price` (per use), `category` (build tab: `furniture`, `structure`, `business`, `decor`), `buildable: false`, `blocks_walking`, `use_position` (`on` or `beside`), `pose`, `action` and `doing` (menu entry and activity), `satisfies` (points restored per hour), `max_use_minutes`, `autonomous: false` (only used on your order), `cures`, `offers_work` (`town` or `lot`), `light` (`color`, `radius` in tiles, `energy`, `shadow`), `smoke`, `connects` and `drag` (walls), `defense`, `flammable` (0 to 1: how readily it burns), `fire_source` (can throw a spark), `accident` (`chance_per_hour`, `death_share`, `text`, `death_cause`: the risk of working at it), `materials` (what it takes to build, by resource id: held materials stand in for their price in coin), and for work sites `gathers` (what a use brings in), `gather_seasons`, `heavy` (not for elders), `drains` (needs lost per hour of work), `skill` (what using it practises and what its level changes), `tool` (a tool the household must hold to use it), `pile: true` (the builders' pile stands beside it), `build_minutes` and `build_skill` (how long and with what it is built; by default from its cost and materials), `sells_animals: true` (a hitching post sells every tame kind), `recipes: true` (a forge or bench: it lists the recipes whose `station` is its id), `sells_tools: true` (a rack that sells every tool). An object with no drawing in `object_art.gd` shows as a crate |
| `needs.json` | `drain_per_hour`, `optional`, `slows_when_empty`, `fatal_after_hours` with a `death_cause`, `seek_below`. A need is only in play while some object or interaction satisfies it |
| `traits.json` | `need_drain_multipliers`, `thought_multipliers`, `group: "orientation"` with `attracted_to` (`opposite`, `same`, `both`, `none`). Everyone gets one orientation and two other traits; `excludes` lists traits that do not go together (the family screen allows three picks); `effects` are read by `Traits` (rest_gain, night_rest_seek, conception, crop_yield, accident, fire_fight_bonus, flee_radius_shift, defense), and polyamorous, faithful, jealous, shy, exhibitionist, coward and gossip are handled in `simulation.gd` |
| `thoughts.json` | State thoughts have a `when` (`need` with `below` or `above`, `idle`, `alone`, `pregnant`, `heavy`, `in_labour`, `partner`, `bond`: `engaged` or `married`, `fulfilled`: has fulfilled an ambition) and hold while it is true; thoughts in one `group` do not stack. Event thoughts have `on` (`used:<object>`, `interaction:<id>`, or an event name) and `hours`. `only_for` and `never_for` take trait ids; `{name}` in the text is the person it is about |
| `interactions.json` | Things two people do: `satisfies`, `max_use_minutes`, `pose` (`talk` or `close`), `opinion` (`gain`, `backfire`, `loss`), `romance` (the same), `min_opinion`, `min_romance`, `adults_only`, `requires_attraction`, `romantic` (family and the taken are out), `begins_dating`, `willing_below` with an `unwilling` reason, `autonomous`, `conception_chance`, `proposes` (an engagement follows), `lesson` (the better skilled teaches the other) |
| `life_stages.json` | `from_age`, `learn` (how fast they learn: 0 for a baby), `walk_multiplier`, `need_drain_multipliers`, `heavy_work: false`, and for the last `dies_between` |
| `lifespans.json` | `days_per_year`: how many game days make a year of life |
| `jobs.json` | `workplace` (a building kind, or a work site: `farm`, `woodlot`, `quarry`, `herb_garden`, `jetty`), `positions`, `wage` per hour, `from` and `to` hours, `station` (the object a worker stands beside), `skill` (what the job practises, if not its station's), `min_age`, `heavy`, `not_for` (classes) |
| `economy.json` | `symbol`, `start_coins`, `npc_start_coins`, `refund_percent`, and prices |

## Systems

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `pregnancy.json` | `duration_years` (of life), trimester and `announce_at` points (as a share of the pregnancy), `labour_hours`, `recovery_years`, `max_mother_age`, `need_drain_multipliers`, `morning_sickness_chance`, `craving_chance`, `twins_chance`, `triplets_chance`, the `complication_*` chances (worse in bed or at the clinic, better with a midwife), `town_conception_chance`, and `baby_care` (the needs a baby has, `tend_below`, `cry_below`, `neglect_hours`, the cradle's hours) |
| `relationships.json` | `ask_out_romance` and `ask_out_opinion` needed, `move_in_romance`, `move_in_dating_days`, `daily_decay`, the date (`date_minutes`, hours, `date_venues`), `breakup_opinion`, and how fast the town's own people pair up and move in; for marriage: what a proposal takes (`propose_romance`, `propose_opinion`, `propose_after_days`), the wedding (`engagement_days`, the hours, `wedding_minutes`, who is a guest, `dress_hours`), the `surname` rule (`wife_takes_husband`, `husband_takes_wife` or `keep`) and how readily the town's couples marry |
| `skills.json` | The skills: `gain` (xp per minute of practice) and `effects`, per level: `yield` (more gathered), `safety` (fewer accidents), `boost` (more from a cooking pot or a lute), `speed` and `save` (faster crafting, less waste), `cure` (nursing lasts longer), `price` (a better price); `deeds` (how many minutes of practice one deed is worth: `treat` for healing, `sale`, `caravan_buy` and `caravan_sale` for trading, `stalk` (each minute) and `kill` for hunting, `ride` (each minute) and `pet` for animal care) |
| `learning.json` | `xp_to_level` (xp for each level, 0 to 10), `ranks`, how much a `talent_bonus` speeds learning and how often talents pass on or turn up, the `master_*` numbers (how far ahead and how near a master must be, and how much faster an apprentice learns), `family_share` and `family_default_xp` (what a youth learns of the family trade), `lesson_xp` |
| `tools.json` | `skill` it serves, shop `price`, `durability` (minutes of use before one breaks) |
| `recipes.json` | `station` (an object with `recipes: true`), `skill`, `min_level`, `inputs` and `outputs` (resources or tools), `minutes` |
| `animals.json` | `type` (`wild` or `domestic`), `pet`, `price`, `move_ticks` (game ticks a step takes: a person's is 3), wild: `habitat` (tile ids), `target` (how many in the world), `alert` (how near a person it notices), `flee_minutes`, `flee_distance`, `hp`, `evade`, `yields` (resources a kill brings), `fights_back` and `hostile` (what a wounded or night-hunting animal does to people: `chance`, `death_share`, `text`, `death_cause`; wolves also `from_hour`, `to_hour`, `radius`, `keep_from_towns`); tame: `ride` (step cost while ridden, 0 for not rideable), `carry` (a share of a gatherer's load it adds when led), `eats` (what it takes from the stockpile) and `ration_days`, `helps_hunt`; `art` (body, head, colours, ears, tail, antlers, horns, tusks, mane, saddle, panniers) |
| `death.json` | When Death appears, arrives and takes, plea chances and what pleading is worth, what the spared get back, who he never spares, how often souls are restless and ghosts walk, and what makes people mourn or gloat |
| `illness.json` | Sicknesses and injuries: `chance_per_day` (0 for injuries, which only accidents cause), `season_multipliers`, `dirty_below` and `dirty_multiplier`, `recovery_per_day`, `rested_bonus`, `herb_bonus`, `fatal_after_days` with a `death_cause`, `need_drain_multipliers`; `contagious` with `spread_per_hour` and `spread_radius`, and `immunity_days` after recovering. An object lists what it heals under `cures` |
| `accidents.json` | `fire`: `spark_chance_per_day` per fire kept (a hearth), how long things `burn_minutes` (more per point of flammability), how often and how readily it spreads, how far folk flee and come to fight it, how much each takes off a fire a minute, and how long standing in it burns and kills. Floors have `flammable` too |
| `conflicts.json` | Raids: `chance_per_day`, `warning_days`, `strength`, announcement texts, plunder, casualties, standing changes. Defence is counted as `realm.json` says |
| `events.json` | `chance` per day (rolled from the seed), `seasons`, `cooldown_days`, `start_hour`, `duration_hours`, `need_drain_multipliers`, `price_multipliers`, `gather` (a building kind) with `restore`. `decree: true` makes a decree, proclaimed only on quarter day |
| `ambitions.json` | A lifetime goal: `name`, `text`, `steps` (each with `text` and a `goal`: one of `deed` (an event key or list of them, with `count`), `skill` (an id or `any`, with `level`), `skills_known` (with `level`), `friends`, `close_friends`, `coins`, `towns`, `partner`, `engaged`, `married`), `weights` (who leans to it: `trait:<id>`, `talent:<skill>` or `talent:any`, `skill:<id>` (level 2 or more), `trade:<skill>` or `trade:any`, `stage:<id>`, `partner`, `single`, each a multiplier), `never_for` (trait ids) and `stages` (the life stages that may take it up) |
| `wishes.json` | A small wish: `text`, `deed` (the event key, or list, that grants it), `hours` (before it lapses), `weights` (as for ambitions), `big` (a bigger lift), `stages`, `never_traits`, and what the world needs to have: `object`, `animals`, `towns` (how many), `partner` (true or false), `employed`, `household`, `skill_level` (with `skill`). `do` is what an idle person does about it: `use` (an object), `interact` (an interaction, with `with: "partner"`), `pet`, `visit` (`npc_only` keeps your folk from setting off on their own) |
| `dreams.json` | The rules: `ambition_stages`, `pick_wait_days`, `wish_stages`, `max_wishes` (`max_wishes_child`), `wish_chance` (per hour), `wish_cooldown_hours`, how idle folk act on wishes (`pursue_every` minutes, `pursue_from_minute`, `pursue_until_minute`, `pursue_reach` in tiles, `pursue_retry` minutes), `friend_opinion`, `close_friend_opinion`, `happy_mood` (a happy day) |
| `families.json` | How the towns carry on: `arrive_chance` and `move_chance` (per empty house per day), `settle_days` (how long a family that has just moved stays put), `couple_chance`, `kids`, `adult_ages` and `kid_ages` of newcomers, `orphan_age`, `chronicle_limit`, `tool_sale_percent` (what a household's tools fetch when you take up another family) |
| `sounds.json` | `recipe` (`noise_bed`, `crickets`, `chirps`, `murmur`, `crackle`, `tone`, `bell`, `thump`) with its numbers (`seconds`, `low_cut`, `high_cut`, `mod_rate`, `freq`, `partials`, `ratios`, `decays`...), `loop`, `volume`. An object names sounds with `ambient` (`id`, `radius` in tiles: heard near it) and `work_sound` (`id`, `every` seconds: made by whoever is using it), and `instrument` (`lute`, `harp` or `drum`: whoever uses it plays a tune) |
| `music.json` | A mood of the music: `mode` (`ionian`, `dorian`, `phrygian`, `mixolydian`, `aeolian`), `root` (a midi note), `bpm`, `bars`, `progression` (scale degrees of the chords), `melody` and `bass` (an instrument: `lute`, `harp`, `flute`, `pad`), `pad`, `drum`, `density` (how busy the tune is), `rest_chance`, `volume`. The moods the game asks for are `day`, `evening`, `night`, `festival` and `sorrow` |
| `clothing.json` | An outfit: `name`, `warmth` (what it does to the drain of warmth: under 1 keeps more heat in), `text`. The ids the game asks for are `undergarments`, `sleepwear`, `casual`, `worker`, `formal`, `warm`, `winter` and `swim` |
| `wardrobe.json` | When each outfit is worn (see `Clothing`): `winter_from` and `warm_from` (how cold it is to the person: the season's warmth drain, less under a roof, more at night), `night_from_hour`, `night_to_hour`, `night_chill`, `formal_objects` (a pew, the altar), `wash_objects`, `work_jobs`, `work_min_stage` |
| `sprites.json` | Painted pictures (see [ART.md](ART.md)): `object.<id>` with `files`, `fit`, `base`, `flip`, `shadow`; `floor.<id>` with `file` and `tiles`; `pawn.<life stage>.<outfit>` with `variants` of `{ front, back }` |
| `starts.json` | The ways to begin: `title`, `class`, `coins`, `house` (a template already standing), `camp`, `tools` and `stock` (what the household starts with), `pets` (animals it begins with) |
| `titles.json`, `classes.json` (also `skills`: what the class tends to know), `guilds.json`, `realm.json` | Land: tax and prices per tile; class rank and tax; guild fee, dues and grants; quarter-day length, death duty, eviction, decrees |
